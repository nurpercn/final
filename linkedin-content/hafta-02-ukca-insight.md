## Post (kopyala-yapıştır)

After Brexit, “CE still works in the UK” became one of the most dangerous sentences in product compliance.

Not because it was always wrong — but because it was incomplete.

When the UKCA transition landed on refrigeration portfolios, the hard part wasn’t the logo change. It was the grey zone:

• Which products could continue under CE, and for how long?
• Where did OPSS guidance stop, and commercial risk begin?
• How do you keep factories shipping while interpretations are still evolving?

What worked for us was treating UKCA as a programme, not a paperwork task:
1. Map the portfolio by risk and market exposure
2. Engage authorities and industry bodies early (not after a customs issue)
3. Create decision rules the business can actually follow
4. Protect continuity — zero drama in production is a compliance KPI

Regulatory transitions don’t fail because teams lack templates.
They fail when ambiguity is left unmanaged.
