# NIBE assessment + feedback görüşmesi

Paket: **BP Basprofil** (kişilik, ~25 dk, ~137 madde) + **g-num** (~10 dk) + **g-fair** (~20 dk).  
Sonra İsveç’te tipik adım: **återkoppling / feedback** — HR veya test kullanıcısı profili seninle konuşur. Amaç “yakalandın mı?” değil; kendini tanıyor musun, örnek verebiliyor musun, rolle uyuyor mu.

Önce testleri bitir, sonra bu dosyanın 3. bölümünü sesli prova et.

---

## 1. Test günü — pratik

- Sessiz oda, şarj, kablolu internet tercih, telefon uçak modu.
- İngilizce talimat seç (Swedish native değilsin; g-fair zaten dil bağımsız).
- Kişilikte süre rahat; mantık testlerinde süre katı. Ara verme, ikinci sekme açma.
- Hesap makinesi: g-num’da çoğu zaman yok / gerekmez. Zihinsel + kâğıt kalem hazırla (izin varsa).
- g-num ve g-fair’de takıldığın soruyu bırak, işaretle, dön. 40 soru / 20 dk ≈ 30 sn.
- Tahmin: şıklıysa boş bırakma; süre bitiyorsa işaretle.
- Aynı oturumda üçünü de istiyorlarsa sıra: kişilik en son (yorgunluk mantığı bozar). Sıra sistemde kilitliyse sisteme uy.

---

## 2. g-num — nasıl çalış

**Ne:** Sayısal *mantık*, ileri matematik değil. ~23 madde, 10 dk. Seri, oran, “hangisi uymaz”, sayı analojisi.

**Her gün 20–30 dk, 3–5 gün:**

1. Sayı dizisi: `2, 6, 18, 54, ?` (×3) · `3, 4, 6, 9, 13, ?` (+1,+2,+3,+4) · `8, 6, 9, 5, 10, 4, ?` (iki iç içe dizi)
2. Oran / yüzde: 40’ın %15’i; 80→100 kaç % artış; 3:5 = x:20
3. Basit işlem hızı: çarpım tablosu, 12×15, 240/16, kesir sadeleştirme
4. Analogi: `3 → 12` ise `5 → ?` (×4) · `2,5 → 10` ise `3 → 12`

**Pratik yerler (Psytest kopyası değil, aynı kas):**

- [123test number sequences](https://www.123test.com/number-sequences-test/)
- [Aptitude-test numerical](https://www.aptitude-test.com/numerical-reasoning.html)
- Telefon: “number series quiz” / mental math

**Test anı kuralı:** ilk 5 soruda tempo tut. 25 sn’de göremediysen geç. Son 1 dakikada boşları doldur.

---

## 3. g-fair — nasıl çalış

**Ne:** Dil yok. Şekil / matris. Raven tarzı. ~40 madde, 20 dk. NIBE seni yabancı dil dezavantajı olmasın diye bunu seçmiş; bu avantajın.

**Ne bakıyorsun (hepsini 5 sn tarama):**

- Sayım: şekil sayısı 1-2-3-4
- Dönme: 90° / 180°
- Ekleme / çıkarma: A + B = C (şekil birleşimi)
- Gölge / doluluk: boş → yarım → dolu
- Satır ve sütun kuralı (3×3 matris): her satırda aynı kural
- Simetri, ayna, iç içe şekil, çizgi sayısı

**Pratik:**

- [123test Raven matrices](https://www.123test.com/raven-matrices-test/) (ücretsiz, en yakın)
- [IQTestLabs abstract](https://www.iqtestlabs.com/) veya “free abstract reasoning test matrices”
- İlk gün: süre yok, kuralı yüksek sesle söyle (“satırda şekil dönüyor, sütunda renk artıyor”)
- Sonra süre koy: 30 sn/soru

**Test anı:** 3×3’te önce satır, sonra sütun, sonra çapraz. İki kural birden olabilir. Şıklar tuzak: doğru kuralın “neredeyse” hali.

## 4. Kişilik (BP Basprofil) — nasıl doldur

~137 madde, ~25 dk. Likert: bana uyar / uymaz. **Normatif** (her madde ayrı). Tutarlılık / sosyal beğenirlik kontrolü vardır.

**Yapma:** Tüm maddelerde “en iyi çalışan”. Feedback’de “profilin çok düzgün, gerçek hayatta nasılsın?” gelir, saparsın.

**Yap:** İş halini düşün, ev halini değil. Aynı özelliğe 4–5 kez farklı cümleyle dönerler — ilk dürüst cevaba sadık kal.

**BP ölçekleri** (raporda bunları konuşurlar):

| Ölçek | Ne | Bu rolde |
|---|---|---|
| Pliktkänsla (görev / duktighet) | Kendinden (ve biraz başkasından) düzgün iş beklemek | Yüksek iyi |
| Ambition | Çıta, tanınma | Orta–yüksek; “ünvan avcısı” değil |
| Energi | Tempo, efor | Orta–yüksek; tükenmiş kahraman değil |
| Sällskaplighet | Grupte çok zaman | Orta; satış rolü değil |
| Påverkan | Öne çıkmak, sosyal kendinden emin | Orta; bağıran lider değil, net duruş |
| Stabilitet | Sakin, eşit ruh hali | Yüksek iyi |
| Anpassning | Uyum, başkasını dikkate alma | Orta–yüksek; “her şeye evet” değil |
| Struktur | Plan, düzen | Yüksek iyi — senin imzan |
| Kreativitet | Orijinal fikir | Orta; kaotik mucit değil |
| Riskbereddhet | Risk / macera aramak | **Düşük–orta iyi** (ürün güvenliği) |

Senin gerçek profil (abartma, inkâr da etme):

- Yapı ve görev duygusu yüksek. Etiket, rapor, stok. Satış bunu başta “engel” sandı.
- İstikrar: 2024 yoğunluğu, sakin kalmak, makine mühendisleri direnci.
- Uyum var ama yasal riskte hayır dersin (Çin üretimi, yanlış numune).
- Sosyal: uluslararası paydaş, toplantı — parti kelebeği değil.
- Etki: resmi unvan olmadan koordine ettin; sahneye çıkmak için değil iş bitsin diye.
- Risk: operasyonel riski *azaltırsın*; mevzuat griyse otoriteye gidersin, kumar oynamazsın.
- Yaratıcılık: sistem, etiket, alternatif sertifika rotası — sanat değil çözüm.
- Ambisyon: derinleşmek, unvan koleksiyonu değil. Vestel hikâyesini “terfi edemedim” diye değil “yapı istedim” diye tut.

Şu tip maddelerde tutarlı ol:

- “I check details even when others think it is unnecessary.” → evet
- “I enjoy taking big risks for excitement.” → hayır
- “I stay calm when plans change.” → evet (örnek hazır)
- “I prefer working alone all the time.” → hayır; koordinasyon var, kalabalık enerji kaynağın değil
- “I always agree to keep the peace.” → hayır
- “I am the first to speak in every meeting.” → hayır

## 5. Feedback görüşmesi — ne olur

45–70 dk, İngilizce. Karşıda Madeleine ve/veya hiring manager / sertifikalı test kullanıcısı.

Akış:

1. Test deneyimin
2. Mantık sonuçları (sayısal / figural)
3. Kişilik ölçek ölçek (yüksek ve düşükler)
4. “Kendini tanıyor musun?” + iş örneği
5. Rolle eşleme (Safety Compliance / belki PED)
6. Gelişim alanı ve nasıl dengelediğin
7. Bazen: görüşmedeki izlenim vs test farkı

**Kazanma kuralı:** sonucu inkâr etme, ezber “güçlü yön” listesi okuma. “Evet, bu bana uyuyor, işte şu olay” veya “skala yüksek görünüyor, işte şöyle sınırlıyorum.”

Düşük g-num/g-fair: panik yok. “Tempo altında sayısal/şekil; işte kanıt ve raporu yavaş ve doğru kontrol ederim. Test hız oyunu, dosya hız oyunu değil.”

Çok yüksek kişilik “mükemmel çalışan”: “Raporda düzgün görünebilir; zayıf noktam erken fazla detay. Sonucu önce söylüyorum.”

## 6. Feedback — gelebilecek tüm soru tipleri

Aşağıda kategoriler. Her birine 30–60 sn İngilizce iskelet. Ezberleme; örneği değiştirme: UK, Çin, G-Mark, 5.000 kod, Körfez FIFO, tasarım ekibi.

### A. Açılış ve test deneyimi

**How did you find the tests? / How are you feeling?**  
> Fine, thank you. The personality part felt straightforward. The timed tests were tight, especially g-fair with many items. I stayed with what I could solve and moved on when I was stuck.

**Did anything surprise you? Was the language OK?**  
> I chose English. g-fair is visual, which is fair if Swedish is not your first language. Nothing felt unrelated to problem-solving under time.

**Did you have a quiet environment? Any interruption?**  
Dürüst ol. Kesildiyse söyle.

**Have you done similar tests before?**  
> Not this publisher. I have done structured assessments and a lot of numerical and statistical work in validation. Timed matrix tests are less part of my daily job.

---

### B. Öz-farkındalık (en çok puan buradan)

**Does this profile feel like you?**  
> Yes in the main picture: structured, duty-driven, calm, not a risk-seeker, more specialist than a loud extravert. If something looks extreme, I would rather talk about how I use it at work than argue with the number.

**What in the report do you recognise most?**  
> Structure and conscientiousness. I will check a rating label to the last digit because that is what fails an audit a year later.

**What do you not recognise / what would you challenge?**  
Sadece gerçekten şaşırırsan.  
> I might come out more introverted than people who see me in meetings. In the job I am direct with labs and authorities. I am not the person who needs a large social group to function.

**If a close colleague read this, what would they say?**  
> They would say I am precise, I do not let a non-compliant unit ship, and I explain the risk instead of just saying no. Early on sales thought I was blocking. Later they trusted the judgement.

**Where could this profile be misread?**  
> High structure can look slow or inflexible. I am not slow on purpose. I am unwilling to release on hope. I say yes when the risk is manageable.

**Did you try to answer as the ‘ideal candidate’?**  
> I answered as I work, not as a poster. For a safety role, pretending to be a high risk-taker would be a bad idea anyway.

---

### C. g-num / g-fair sonuçları

**Your numerical / figural score is [high/average/below]. How do you see that?**

Yüksek:  
> That matches how I like patterns and numbers. In the job I still slow down for the file: the test rewards speed, the technical file rewards being right.

Orta:  
> I am comfortable with numbers from validation and energy data. A 10-minute test is not the same as a gap analysis I can sit with. I double-check calculations that go on a label.

Düşük:  
> I would not hide it. Timed multiple-choice is not how I do compliance. My work is standards, reports, and cross-checks. I use tables, colleagues, and time when the decision is safety-critical. I learn fast when the material is in front of me — that is also how I moved from chemistry into this field.

**How do you work with numerical data in your job?**  
> Energy classes, rated current, production volumes, test budgets, measurement uncertainty in my earlier role. I do not guess a current on a rating plate.

**When a result is unexpected, what do you do?**  
> Stop, check the sample, the method, the standard edition. In China I would not send a unit I already knew would fail.

**g-fair is culture-fair. Any comment?**  
> It is a reasonable choice for an international candidate. I am used to reading patterns in standards and product construction, which is a similar muscle, just not under a 30-second clock.

### D. BP ölçek ölçek — yüksek çıkarsa / düşük çıkarsa

Her ölçek için: “Bu sende yüksek/düşük görünüyor, örnek ver.” İki yönlü hazırlan.

**Struktur (yapı)**  
Yüksek — beklenen.  
> I built monthly certificate trackers because the factory had none. I plan transitions backwards from the legal date: test, parts, stock, shipment.

Düşük görünürse (şaşırtıcı):  
> My days are interrupt-driven, so the calendar looks messy. The *system* is structured: lists, owners, dates. I would own that difference.

**Pliktkänsla (görev / doğruluk)**  
> I held Chinese production until registration and the new energy label existed. Unpopular. Correct.

Ters soru: *When is good enough, good enough?*  
> When the file, the mark and the unit match, and the residual risk is understood. Not when sales wants the date.

**Stabilitet (sakinlik)**  
> 2024 was heavy: several market transitions, a new colleague, my qualifying exam. I kept the certifications moving. I do not do drama in the factory.

Ters: *Do you under-react?*  
> I go quiet and list the facts. People sometimes need the conclusion first. I have learned to say ship / don’t ship before the clause numbers.

**Anpassning (uyum)**  
> I adapt the explanation to sales, R&D, a notified body, OPSS. I do not adapt the legal requirement to the loudest stakeholder.

Ters: *Are you difficult?*  
> I am difficult only on non-compliance that we cannot defend. I spend most of my time finding a legal way.

**Ambition**  
> I want to go deep on heat-pump safety at a manufacturer that stays with people. I am not collecting titles. At Vestel I did the lead job without a stable title; I left because the structure was not honest, not because I needed a bigger business card.

**Energi**  
> I can run a high load. I also know that 100-country firefighting is not the life I want next. Markaryd depth fits better than permanent crisis mode.

**Sällskaplighet**  
> I work all day with people. I recharge with focused work. A specialist role next to a lab suits me better than a role that is only meetings.

**Påverkan (etki / öne çıkma)**  
> I will take the room when the regulation is unclear — UK, GSO, G-Mark tender. I do not need to be the most visible person in the canteen.

Ters: *Can you push back on senior people?*  
> Yes, with sources. UKCA I would not push on opinion; I went to OPSS and AMDEA.

**Kreativitet**  
> I invent processes, not products: alternative certification routes, a packing label so 300,000 units could not mix, a scheduling model for test chambers. I am not the person who wants to redesign the heat pump from a blank page.

**Riskbereddhet**  
Yüksek çıkmamalı; çıkarsa düzelt:  
> I take *process* risk: talking to an authority, changing a lab, holding production. I do not take product-safety risk. Those are different.

Düşük (uygun):  
> For this job I would be worried if I scored like a trader. I like controlled transitions, not surprises in the field.

### E. Rolle eşleme (Safety / PED / Markaryd)

**How does this profile fit a Safety Compliance Engineer?**  
> The job needs someone who will not release a file that does not match the unit, who can stay calm with R&D and labs, and who can say no without making it personal. That is how I already work on IEC 60335 and CE. Heat-pump 2-40 I will learn; the behaviour is not new.

**Where might you struggle here?**  
> Swedish as a working language over time. PED and 60335-2-40 content on day one. Hands-on in *your* lab until I learn the house method. Socially, a small town is fine; I am not looking for a city networking job.

**You applied to two similar roles. What does the profile say?**  
> Safety is the honest match: structure, electrical safety, files. PED I can grow into with the team. I would not fake a high ‘mechanical risk appetite’.

**Independence vs team?**  
> I work independently on the analysis. I do not disappear: labs, R&D, production have to see the same plan.

**How do you handle disagreement with designers?**  
> Construction vs clause, then options. I led mechanical engineers without being one. I did not fake coil design. I made the certification constraint visible.

**Detail vs speed to market?**  
> Parallel: start the file while design is still moving; freeze only what must wait. That is how you get both.

**Relocation / work permit / Swedish?**  
Kısa, daha önce söylediğin gibi. Test feedback’inde tekrar sorabilirler.

### F. Stres, çatışma, hata, etik

**Describe yourself under pressure.**  
> Lists, owners, legal dates first. UK and Gulf transitions. I do not send a hopeful sample to the lab.

**A time you were wrong.**  
> Gulf looked successful; supply and FIFO could have broken it. I used that on the UK: earlier technical date, stock plan, visual pack mark.

**Conflict with sales.**  
> Labels. They thought I was pedantic. One wrong current fails inspection. Trust came later.

**Unpopular no.**  
> China: wrong sample, no production without the new label.

**Integrity: someone asks you to ship anyway.**  
> I explain the downside in their language: customs, recall, customer penalty. I do not sign a DoC I cannot defend. If the company insists, that is not a company I should join — I do not believe NIBE is that company.

**How do you recover from a bad day?**  
> Finish the critical path item, write down the rest, sleep. I do not send angry mails.

### G. Liderlik, geri bildirim, gelişim

**Do you want to manage?**  
> I can lead a small technical group. This role is an individual contributor. That is what I am applying for.

**How do you take critical feedback?**  
> I want the specific example. “Too detailed” — I now lead with the decision. I do not collapse.

**Development areas we should know?**  
> 1) Lead with the conclusion. 2) Heat-pump 2-40 and PED content. 3) Swedish. 4) I can over-own problems that sit in supply or the warehouse — I now design for that, not only for the procedure.

**How do you mentor?**  
> I trained a colleague with no certification background, and new sales people by market. Short, practical, not a standard dump.

### H. Bilişsel stil / karar

**Fast vs slow decisions?**  
> Fast when the facts are enough (this sample is out of spec). Slow when the law is grey (UK marking vs safety requirement). I do not mix those.

**Big picture vs detail?**  
> I start from the market and the date, then I go to the label. Both. The failure mode is staying in the label too long before I say the date.

**How do you learn a new standard?**  
> Scope, definitions, Part 1 vs Part 2, then gap against our construction, then test plan. I would do that with 2-40 in Markaryd.

**Data vs gut?**  
> Gut tells me where to look. I do not ship on gut.

### I. Kültür / İsveç / NIBE değerleri

**Simplicity, humility, reliability — where are you?**  
> Reliability is the job. Humility: I will not claim PED or 2-40 I do not have. Simplicity: a monthly expiry list beats a perfect system nobody uses.

**How do you work with quieter Swedish communication?**  
> I can leave space. I will still be explicit on a safety stop. I would rather be clear than polite and ambiguous on a legal risk.

**Family-owned / industrial town.**  
> I have always worked next to production. That is the point of Markaryd.

### J. Tutarlılık tuzakları (bilerek sorarlar)

**Your test says highly structured; in the interview you sounded flexible. Which is true?**  
> Both. The *rules I will not bend* are legal. The *path* can be alternative lab, family grouping, starting the file early.

**Your test says low sociability; you coordinate 100 countries.**  
> Coordination is the work, not a preference for large groups. I write, I call, I close. Then I go back to the file.

**You seem very certain. Are you open to being wrong?**  
> UK I was certain enough to ask the authority, not certain enough to skip them. If Markaryd’s house rule differs, I learn it before I change it.

**Everything you say sounds like a strength. Give a real weakness.**  
> I over-process “what if” after a project that others already called a success. Gulf. It made UK better and it tired me. I now put the what-if into the plan *before* the date, then I stop rumination.

**Why should we trust a self-report test?**  
> You shouldn’t on its own. That is why you talk to me and to my manager. Use the profile as a map, not a verdict.
