\subsection{Chamber assignment under environment constraints}
\label{subsec:chamber_assignment}

For a fixed sample vector \(\mathbf{s}\), this stage sets each
chamber to one temperature--humidity environment.
Tests are assigned later by the scheduler.
Pull-down work scales with \(\mathbf{s}\), so the assignment is
recomputed whenever the sample vector changes.

Let \(\mathcal{P}^v=\{p\in\mathcal{P}:\nu_p=1\}\).
The workload \(W_e(\mathbf{s})\) is already defined; voltage-sensitive
work is the same sum over \(\mathcal{P}^v\) only:
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:workload_voltage}
W_e^V(\mathbf{s})
=
\sum_{p\in\mathcal{P}^v}
\sum_{\substack{t\in\mathcal{T}_p\\e(t)=e}}
m_{pt}(\mathbf{s})\tau_t.
\end{equation}
Only environments with \(W_e(\mathbf{s})>0\) are used below.
With \(M^{tot}=\sum_{c\in\mathcal{C}}M_c\) and
\(M^{V,tot}=\sum_{c\in\mathcal{C}}M_cv_c\),
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:targets}
T_e
=
\frac{W_e(\mathbf{s})}{\sum_{e'}W_{e'}(\mathbf{s})}\,M^{tot},
\qquad
T_e^V
=
\frac{W_e^V(\mathbf{s})}{\sum_{e'}W_{e'}^V(\mathbf{s})}\,M^{V,tot},
\end{equation}
and \(T_e^V=0\) whenever \(W_e^V(\mathbf{s})=0\).
The assignment model is
\begin{align}
\min\quad
&
\sum_{e\in\mathcal{E}}z_e+\sum_{e\in\mathcal{E}}z_e^V
\label{eq:assign_obj}\\
\text{s.t.}\quad
&
\sum_{e\in\mathcal{E}}x_{ce}=1
&& \forall c\in\mathcal{C},
\label{eq:assign_one}\\
&
\sum_{c\in\mathcal{C}}x_{ce}\ge 1
&& \forall e\in\mathcal{E},
\label{eq:assign_cover}\\
&
\sum_{c\in\mathcal{C}}v_cx_{ce}\ge 1
&& \forall e\in\mathcal{E}:W_e^V(\mathbf{s})>0,
\label{eq:assign_volt}\\
&
x_{ce}=0
&& \text{if }e\text{ needs humidity and }h_c=0,
\label{eq:assign_hum}\\
&
S_e=\sum_{c\in\mathcal{C}}M_cx_{ce},
\quad
V_e=\sum_{c\in\mathcal{C}}M_cv_cx_{ce}
&& \forall e\in\mathcal{E},
\label{eq:assign_counts}\\
&
z_e\ge |S_e-T_e|,
\quad
z_e^V\ge |V_e-T_e^V|
&& \forall e\in\mathcal{E},
\label{eq:assign_dev}\\
&
x_{ce}\in\{0,1\},\quad S_e,V_e,z_e,z_e^V\ge 0.
&&
\label{eq:assign_dom}
\end{align}

The integer program matches station counts to \((T_e,T_e^V)\).
Tardiness is corrected afterwards.
Let \((\Pi,T^{tot})=\mathrm{Schedule}(\mathbf{x},\mathbf{s})\) and
let \(B_c\) be the total processing time placed in chamber \(c\) by
\(\Pi\). The load of chamber \(c\) is
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:chamber_load}
\lambda_c=\frac{B_c}{M_c}.
\end{equation}
A swap \(\mathbf{x}^{c\leftrightarrow c'}\) exchanges the
environments of \(c\) and \(c'\) when
\(\lambda_c>\lambda_{c'}\) and both chambers can hold the other's
setting.
A move \(\mathbf{x}^{c\rightarrow c'}\) copies the environment of
the heavier chamber onto the lighter one.
Both maps are kept only if they satisfy
\eqref{eq:assign_one}--\eqref{eq:assign_hum}.
Pairs are scanned from the largest \(\lambda_c\) to the smallest;
the first neighbor with a strictly smaller \(T^{tot}\) is accepted.
Swap is tried first; move is used only when no swap improves.
Algorithm~\ref{alg:chamber_assignment} is the procedure.

\begin{algorithm}[H]
\caption{ChamberAssignment}
\label{alg:chamber_assignment}
\begin{algorithmic}[1]
\State \textbf{Input:} sample vector \(\mathbf{s}\)
\State \textbf{Output:} chamber assignment \(\mathbf{x}\)
\State Compute \(W_e(\mathbf{s})\), \(W_e^V(\mathbf{s})\), \(T_e\), and \(T_e^V\) \label{step:ca-load}
\State \(\mathbf{x}\leftarrow\) the optimal solution of \eqref{eq:assign_obj}--\eqref{eq:assign_dom} \label{step:ca-exact}
\Repeat \label{step:ca-loop}
    \State Compute \(\lambda_c\) from \(\mathrm{Schedule}(\mathbf{x},\mathbf{s})\) \label{step:ca-loadrank}
    \State Try swaps \(\mathbf{x}^{c\leftrightarrow c'}\) on pairs with \(\lambda_c>\lambda_{c'}\) \label{step:ca-nbhd}
    \If{no improving swap}
        \State Try moves \(\mathbf{x}^{c\rightarrow c'}\) on the same pairs
    \EndIf
    \If{a neighbor improves \(T^{tot}\)} \label{step:ca-accept}
        \State Replace \(\mathbf{x}\) by that neighbor
    \EndIf
\Until{no improving feasible neighbor remains} \label{step:ca-stop}
\State \Return \(\mathbf{x}\)
\end{algorithmic}
\end{algorithm}

Step~\ref{step:ca-load} forms the loads and the targets.
Step~\ref{step:ca-exact} solves the assignment model.
Step~\ref{step:ca-loadrank} ranks chambers by
\eqref{eq:chamber_load}.
Steps~\ref{step:ca-nbhd}--\ref{step:ca-accept} accept the first
feasible heaviest--lightest swap, or move, that reduces tardiness,
and stop when a pass finds none (Step~\ref{step:ca-stop}).
