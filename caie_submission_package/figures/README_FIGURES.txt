Place your figure file here as:

  chamber_structure.png

The anonymized manuscript expects:

  \includegraphics[width=1\textwidth]{figures/chamber_structure.png}

Requirements (Elsevier artwork guidance):
- Prefer TIFF/PNG/JPG for photos/schematics at sufficient resolution
- Line art ideally >= 1000 dpi; photos >= 300 dpi
- Do not embed author names, logos of your university, or acknowledgements in the figure
- Caption stays in the manuscript, not on the image itself if possible

Until the real figure is added, compilation will fail on the missing image.
You can temporarily comment out the figure environment for a draft compile.
